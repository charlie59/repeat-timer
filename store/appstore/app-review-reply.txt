2. Purpose and audience: Yoga & Egg Timer is a simple interval timer with very large numbers and buttons, readable from a yoga mat or across a kitchen. Its main audience is Kundalini yoga practitioners, whose kriyas repeat the same timed interval several times with a short rest in between; existing timers make this fiddly. REPEAT cycles one interval with a configurable rest gap, RESTART restarts the current round, and cue sounds play even with the screen locked. It is equally useful as a plain kitchen timer.

3. Setup and access: No account, login, or sample files. Launch the app, tap the time to set it, and tap START. Tap REPEAT to use repeating rounds; this opens the unlock sheet, where START FREE TRIAL begins a 7-day trial (trial_7day, $0 non-consumable) and UNLOCK buys the full version once (unlock_full, $2.99). Restore purchase is on the same sheet. Allow notifications so cue sounds play while the screen is locked (they are scheduled local notifications).

4. External services: Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit 2) for the trial and unlock. Apple UserNotifications for on-device local notifications. No servers, analytics, advertising, AI services, or third-party SDKs; no data is collected or transmitted.

5. Regional differences: None. The app works identically in all regions; only the App Store price is localized by Apple.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material: Not applicable. All sounds and artwork are our own.
